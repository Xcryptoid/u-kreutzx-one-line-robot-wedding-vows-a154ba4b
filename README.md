One line of wedding vows a robot couple could say, for make.fun's "One-Line Robot Wedding Vows" bounty.

The vow (136 characters):

> I take you to be my lawfully wedded co-processor, to have and to hold, in sickness and in low battery, until a factory reset do us part.

`main.py` is a stdlib-only copier: it copies the pre-written `entry.txt` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.

## How the vow maps to the judge's criteria, in order

1. **One-line robot wedding vow that lands as a joke** — it is a vow one robot speaks to another at their wedding, not a generic joke.
2. **The laugh is in the line itself** — the turn is "until a factory reset do us part", swapping "death" for "factory reset" in the final words.
3. **Sounds like something said at a wedding** — "I take you to be my lawfully wedded…", "to have and to hold", "in sickness and in…", "until … do us part": the full traditional vow formula, with a promise in it.
4. **The machine side is specific** — co-processor, low battery, factory reset; the joke would not work for a human couple.
5. **Fits one line and reads well aloud** — the rhythm mirrors the traditional vow, so the laugh lands on the last phrase.
